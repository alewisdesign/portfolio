DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Truth

I dig past the surface ask to find the real problem. Understanding constraints and root causes leads to better decisions than guessing at solutions.

Care

Good design is made with care, and cares about the user. Craft shows in the details; empathy shows in never making someone fight the product.

Impact

Design should produce real outcomes, for the user and the business. If it doesn't move the needle, it's not done.
